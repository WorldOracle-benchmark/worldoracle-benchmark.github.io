Privacy Notice

Version 1.014 October 2026

This notice summarizes the information used to operate the WorldOracle account and evaluation service.

Information used by the portal

This may include registration email, team and model details, organization, logo, public resource links, review notes, sign-in and security records, submission history, evaluation logs, and support correspondence.

Why it is used

The information supports email verification, account review, secure sign-in, submission processing, benchmark auditing, service notices, and publication of approved leaderboard information.

Public and private information

Model, team, organization, logo, approved resources, scores, and selected benchmark samples may appear publicly. Registration email, password hash, private notes, original packages, and internal review records are not intended for public display.

Storage and retention

Passwords are handled as password hashes rather than displayed as plain text. Account, submission, and audit records are kept for as long as reasonably needed to operate and verify the benchmark. A participant may ask the organizers to review or remove information, subject to records needed for security and benchmark integrity.

Avoid placing personal information, credentials, or private tokens in registration notes or submission packages.
