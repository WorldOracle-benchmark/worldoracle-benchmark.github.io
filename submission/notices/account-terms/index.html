Account

Account Terms

Version 1.014 October 2026

These terms explain the basic expectations for using the WorldOracle submission portal.

Account information

  • Use accurate team, model, organization, and contact information.
  • One registration email is linked to one model account. A team may register more than one model through separate accounts.
  • Keep sign-in details private and let the organizers know if an account may have been accessed without permission.

Approvals and access

Email verification and organizer approval are required before formal submissions are available. Access may be paused when information needs clarification, an account appears unsafe, or benchmark rules may have been breached.

Reasonable use

Please do not impersonate another team, interfere with the service, bypass submission limits, or use another participant's account without permission.

Updates

These terms may be refined as the benchmark develops. Material changes will be presented in the portal before they apply to new account or submission actions.

Questions about an account can be raised with the WorldOracle organizers through the contact channel published with the benchmark.
