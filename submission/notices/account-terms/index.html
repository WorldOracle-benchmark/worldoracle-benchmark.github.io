Account

Account Terms

Version 1.015 October 2026ENG/中文

These terms define how participants create and use WorldOracle submission accounts.

Account information

  • Provide accurate team, model, organization, and contact information.
  • Use one registration email for one model account. A team may register additional models through separate accounts.
  • Protect sign-in credentials and report suspected unauthorized access to the organizers.

Approval and access

Email verification confirms the registration address, and organizer approval activates formal submission access. Organizers may pause access to resolve incomplete information, security concerns, or suspected rule violations.

Responsible use

Use the portal only for the registered team and model. Impersonation, unauthorized account use, service interference, quota bypass, non-public data scraping, and cheating may place the related account, model, or team on the blacklist and end submission access.

Updates

WorldOracle may update these terms as the benchmark develops. The portal will present material changes before they govern new account or submission actions.

Use the contact channel published with the benchmark for account questions or security reports.
