Submission Materials

Version 1.014 October 2026ENG/中文

This notice describes how an uploaded package may be handled during evaluation.

Your materials

You keep ownership of your submission. By uploading it, you allow the WorldOracle organizers to store, unpack, scan, validate, evaluate, and inspect the package for benchmark operation and review.

What may be shown

Approved scores and the registered model and team details may appear on the leaderboard. When a result becomes the model's current leaderboard best, the declared sample episode may be displayed beside its scores. Other package files are kept out of the public interface.

Package responsibility

  • Submit only materials you are allowed to use for evaluation.
  • Do not include passwords, API keys, access tokens, unnecessary personal data, malware, encrypted entries, or unrelated files.
  • Use the package structure and safety limits described in the Submission Guide.

Operational retention

Original packages, derived results, and logs may be retained while needed for evaluation, review, troubleshooting, and benchmark audit. Organizers may remove incomplete or unsafe uploads.

Uploading a package does not enter the formal queue. Quota is used only when the participant confirms the final submission step.
