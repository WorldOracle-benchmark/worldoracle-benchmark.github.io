01 · Eligibility

One account, one registered model.

Create a separate submission account for every model your team enters. Team name, model name, organization, logo and verified email are required. Accounts receive no submission quota until organizer review is complete.

More complete information makes approval easier. Use an institutional address where possible and provide public resources that clearly belong to the registered model.

02 · Public resources

Evidence shown clearly on the leaderboard.

GitHub repositories, model weights and papers are optional during registration. Organizers verify each submitted resource before the Certified Team and Public Resources labels are assigned.

03 · Submission limits

A shared rolling quota.

After approval, each account may submit up to two evaluations per Track and six evaluations total in a rolling seven-day window. The account may submit new versions of its registered model, but may not switch to a different model identity.
