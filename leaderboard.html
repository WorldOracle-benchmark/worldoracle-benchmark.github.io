02 / Action-Source Distribution

Policy Track

AgiBot G2 · Best Results

The Policy Track uses recorded action trajectories collected while a policy interacts with the physical AgiBot G2 robot in closed loop. During evaluation, each trajectory is replayed open loop by the ACWM and compared with its paired real execution.

Single-View Score is the mean of PSC, KIAF and ISA. Multi-View Score adds MVAC with equal weight and is shown only for three-view submissions. Each model keeps its best single-view result; a dash means not applicable.

Scoring reference

Execution coherence, resolved into four dimensions.

WorldOracle compares every prediction with its paired real-world rollout. The first three dimensions use the primary top_head view; multi-view action coherence uses all three synchronized views.

01 · Outcome state

Physical State Correctness

Checks whether predicted outcome keyframes satisfy the physical-state criteria annotated from the paired real execution.

02 · Spatial correspondence

Interaction Spatial Alignment

Measures whether manipulated objects and robot arms or human hands remain aligned with their real-world trajectories.

03 · Local appearance

Key Interaction Area Fidelity

Evaluates pixel, perceptual, and semantic fidelity inside action-relevant object and arm or hand regions.

04 · Synchronized views

Multi-View Action Coherence

Measures robot-execution alignment against the paired real rollout across synchronized top, left-hand, and right-hand views.
